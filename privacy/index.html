What we collect and why

When you ask for a quote, book a snagging inspection or ask for a callback, we get the details you type in: your name, phone number, email, town, the work you want, your budget, and for snagging your development, builder, plot number and dates. We use them only to reply, quote, book and carry out the job. Our reason in law is that you asked us to take steps before a contract.

The forms are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Snagging bookings and callbacks go to our inbox, ready4refurb@gmail.com. Quote requests from the main form are delivered to info@battleplanmedia.co.uk.

We keep enquiries that do not become jobs for up to 12 months. For jobs we carry out, we keep records for 6 years for tax and warranty reasons.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies unless you say yes. We ask once whether we can use the Facebook (Meta) pixel, which tells us which of our Facebook and Instagram adverts brought people here. If you accept, Meta Platforms Ireland receives details of your visit and may combine them with your Facebook account. If you reject, nothing from Facebook loads. Your choice is saved in your own browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps short-term server logs, including IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, or object to how we use them. Email ready4refurb@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
