Common questions

What is the difference between pre completion and post completion?

A pre completion inspection happens before you legally complete, so the builder can put snags right before you move in. A post completion inspection happens after you have the keys, while the builder is still responsible for fixing defects.

Who does the inspection?

An independent RPSA-qualified inspector who works for you, not the builder.

When do I get my report?

Your digital report arrives within 24 hours of the inspection, ready to send to your builder.

How much does a snagging inspection cost?

A pre completion inspection costs from £325 for a 1 to 2 bed apartment to £550 for a 5 bed+ house. Booking both inspections costs from £575 to £750.

When should I book?

Book a pre completion inspection as soon as you have your completion date, so the builder has time to put snags right before you move in.

I have already moved in. Is it too late?

No. Book a post completion inspection. Most new build warranties, such as NHBC Buildmark, make the builder responsible for fixing defects reported in the first two years, so book within that time.
